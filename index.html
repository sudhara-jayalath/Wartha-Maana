warthamaana the present

Written whole, warthamaana is the present. Split in two, the same letters read as twisted dimensions. Wartha Maana is a record label and publication house. See the artists, read the writing, reserve a book.

The split holds in Sanskrit too. Vartamāna is vṛt, to turn or to exist, with the participle ending -māna, so the present is whatever is turning. Latin vertere and German werden share that root, and obstetricians still call the turning of an unborn child version.

  • Pahath thanin yai gala jale

    Fragment, on a proverb

    Water seeks the low place. People mostly use the saying as a moral remark about misfortune, yet it also holds as a law of the land, and an engineer, a carpenter and a monk would each hear something different in it. Read that way, Sri Lanka’s yearly floods are about planning as much as climate.

    SagaraWeeratunga(Paty)

    About

    Sagara Weeratunga is a Sri Lankan musician, singer-songwriter, and composer. He serves as the frontman and founder of the rock band R!ddlemach!ne from Gampaha District, which he formed in 2000.

    The band gained widespread popular recognition with their hit single “Eka Bara Husmak” (one heavy breath). Sagara’s solo discography also includes the acclaimed track “Maname.”

    His latest English release, “Waiting,” serves as the lead single for his upcoming concept album, “CREATURE IN ME.”

    Known as Paty, he was also part of Wal.bo. He is the founder and owner of Lythium Records and of Project Acoustica, a YouTube-based platform for Sri Lanka’s underground musicians and artists.

    අප ගැනAbout us

    Wartha Maana is the record label and publication house I envisioned years ago.

    The name is one word split in two. Written whole, warthamaana is the present. Split, the same letters read as twisted dimensions.

    The label supports my fellow Wal.bo members and our creative community in their own careers. The publication house starts with two limited editions of 50 copies each: මමංකාර and the Wal.bo lyrics. There is also a handmade Wal.bo lyric book, limited to 10 pieces.

    For now, Wartha Maana is me, so every message comes straight to me.

    Sudhara Jayalath

    Wal.bo was my band.

    An underground folk punk band, mainly acoustic and unplugged. It began at the University of Moratuwa in 2014 and stayed active until the beginning of 2018.

    Never recorded.

    Wal.bo treated its music as folk music. Authorship is not what matters, the band claimed, but the medium through which the music travels.

    Both photos: a gig at the NDT campus, ITUM, Diyagama, 2017.

    There are still places in Sri Lanka where you can hear someone playing these songs at a small gathering, smoking pot or having a drink.
